A First-Time Homebuyer's Guide to Utah

By Nick Gonzalez, Keller Williams

Buying your first home is exciting, and it can also feel overwhelming. The good news: when you break it into steps, the process is very manageable. Here's how I walk first-time buyers through it.

1. Get pre-approved first

Before you start touring homes, talk to a lender and get pre-approved. A pre-approval tells you what you can comfortably afford and shows sellers you're a serious buyer. In a competitive market, many sellers won't consider an offer without one.

2. Ask about first-time buyer programs

Utah has state and local programs that can help with down payment and closing costs, and many lenders offer low-down-payment loan options. Eligibility depends on income, price, and location, so ask your lender which ones you qualify for. I'm happy to connect you with lenders who work with these programs regularly.

3. Decide what matters most

Make a short list of must-haves (bedrooms, commute, school district) and nice-to-haves (a big yard, a newer kitchen). Knowing the difference helps you move quickly when the right home comes up.

4. Tour homes and make an offer

Once we find the one, I'll pull comparable sales so your offer is grounded in real data, and we'll decide together on price, earnest money, and terms that make your offer stand out.

5. Inspections, appraisal, and closing

After your offer is accepted, you'll have time for inspections and the lender's appraisal. I'll keep track of every contract deadline so nothing slips, and walk you through the final numbers before you sign.
