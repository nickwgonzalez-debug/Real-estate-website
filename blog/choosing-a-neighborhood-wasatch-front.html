How to Choose the Right Neighborhood on the Wasatch Front

By Nick Gonzalez, Keller Williams

From Ogden to Provo, the Wasatch Front offers a lot of great places to live, each with its own feel. Here's what I encourage buyers to think about when narrowing down where to look.

Test your commute

Drive the route to work at the time you'd actually be commuting. Also look at access to I-15, Bangerter, and FrontRunner or TRAX if you'd use transit. A few miles can make a big difference at rush hour.

Look into schools

Even if you don't have kids, school boundaries affect resale value. Check which schools a home is assigned to and verify boundaries with the district, since they can change.

Understand HOAs

Many newer Utah communities have HOAs. Review the monthly dues, what they cover, and the rules on things like parking, fencing, and rentals before you fall in love with a home.

Think about lifestyle

Do you want to be close to canyon trailheads, ski resorts, shopping, or a walkable downtown? Spend a weekend in the areas you're considering to get a feel for daily life.

Plan for the future

Ask about nearby development, road projects, and how the area has grown. I can pull recent sales data to show how different neighborhoods have held their value.
