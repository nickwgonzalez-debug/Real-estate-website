5 Ways to Prep Your Home Before You List

By Nick Gonzalez, Keller Williams

First impressions happen online now. Most buyers see your home's photos long before they walk through the door, so a little prep before listing day goes a long way.

1. Declutter and depersonalize

Clear countertops, thin out closets, and put away family photos. Buyers want to picture their own life in the space, and less stuff makes every room feel bigger.

2. Handle small repairs

Leaky faucets, sticky doors, and burnt-out bulbs are easy fixes, but buyers notice them. Taking care of them up front can prevent inspection headaches later.

3. Freshen up with paint

A fresh coat of neutral paint is one of the most cost-effective updates you can make. It brightens rooms and gives buyers a clean slate.

4. Boost curb appeal

Mow the lawn, trim shrubs, add a few potted plants, and make sure the front door and house numbers look sharp. It's the first thing buyers see in photos and in person.

5. Let in the light

Open blinds, clean windows, and swap in bright bulbs. Light, airy rooms photograph better and feel more welcoming at showings.
